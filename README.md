### 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a Computer Science Engineering student at St. Joseph's College of Engineering and Technology, Palai (raised in Dubai, now based in Kerala), focused on systems programming, developer tooling, and workflow automation.

Most software I build starts with a simple question:

> **"Can this be engineered to be faster, cleaner, or automated entirely?"**

I bridge systems-level networking with practical developer tools:
- **Systems & Networking:** Working close to the wire—handling low-level socket programming, UDP multicast discovery, and concurrent thread pipelines to connect devices directly across LAN subnets without cloud intermediaries.
- **Developer Tools & Automation:** Building services that eliminate tedious manual workflows, from dynamically parsing complex BIM coordination matrices to computing student attendance thresholds entirely on the client side.

Outside of software architecture, I maintain and ride my motorcycle through the Western Ghats, prototype hardware with repurposed RC powertrains, and shoot manual astrophotography. Different mediums, same instinct: diagnosing constraints, tuning mechanisms, and building things that work reliably in the real world.

> 💡 **Targeting:** Systems Software, Backend Engineering, Developer Tooling & Automation Roles
